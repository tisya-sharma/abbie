01 · The mark
One geometry, two treatments, split at 48 px
An immunoglobulin drawn with a hinge bend and a heavier Fc stem. Upright always — that is what makes it legible as an antibody rather than a letter; rotating loses the reference without gaining another. The two-tone light chains are a large-size treatment of the same paths, not a second mark.
Expressive · 48 px and up
Light chains at 62 % opacity. Shown at 96 and 60 px. Use for hero art, the panel greeting, slides, stickers, print.
Solid · below 48 px
One colour, same paths. Shown at 44, 30 and 22 px. Use for the launcher, header avatar, activity mark, favicons, flat print.
Below 48 px the light-chain segment is about two pixels of lighter red on red and reads as a rendering error; the hinge still reads at 22 px. Ship two SVGs, mark-expressive and mark-solid, differing only by two opacity attributes.

The rule that matters. Abstain, redirect and refuse must never show evidence language. Those replies carry no sources, so "Gathering sources…" would promise something the answer cannot deliver — the one way a status line can lie. Neutral opener only.
Rotation as fallback. If the route frame is slow or form is null, cycle the neutral openers rather than freeze — "Thinking…", "Getting oriented…". On a long working stage one second phrase may follow; never more, or it reads as stalling.
06 · Composer
Filled block with a hint row
No orange. At rest the field is a soft filled block with a hairline and a dormant grey send. On focus it goes white, takes an ink ring, and the send wakes up red — the accent means "you're typing", and it lives on the button rather than as a coloured outline. Two lines tall so the keyboard hint has somewhere to live, which is the whole reason this won: Shift + Enter has to be teachable, and a research assistant gets long multi-line questions.

No label pill and no timed nudge on mobile — a card covering content on a small screen is intrusive, and there is no room for it beside the button.
Trap 1 · the composer must be 16 px
iOS Safari zooms the whole page when a focused input is under 16 px, and it does not zoom back out. The composer input is therefore 16 px on mobile, not the 14 px used on desktop. Everything else keeps its desktop size.
Trap 2 · 100vh is wrong on mobile
Mobile browser chrome makes 100vh taller than the visible area, so a 100vh sheet hides its own composer behind the keyboard. Use 100dvh and pad the composer with env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
Touch targets
The action row's 28 px squares become 44 px on touch, and the row is always visible — there is no hover to reveal it. Icons stay 14–15 px; only the tappable area grows. Send button goes 28 → 34 px.
Everything that does not change
Answers stay 15 px / 1.6, sources keep the byline-over-title row capped at three, the mark keeps the solid treatment at every mobile size, and the activity states are identical. One spec, one breakpoint at 640 px.

09 · Errors and notices
Quiet, ink-first, never red
Red is IPI's brand colour — it's on the launcher, the send button, every citation numeral. So a red error block would read as brand furniture rather than a warning, and worse, it makes an ordinary failure feel institutional. Notices are ink on a neutral fill with a single amber rule; red stays reserved for Abbie herself.

Partial text is kept, not discarded — it may still be useful — but it is explicitly marked incomplete so nobody quotes a half sentence as guidance. Sources and the action row are suppressed on an incomplete answer.
Abstain, redirect and refuse are not errors
Those three routes are Abbie working correctly — declining a named-reagent question, steering back on topic, refusing clinical use. They render as ordinary answers in normal ink, with no notice styling and no sources block. Styling them as errors would tell visitors the product is broken when it is behaving exactly as designed.
Notice anatomy
Fill #FBFBF9, 2 px left rule #C98A28, radius 0 4 4 0, padding 12/14. Body 13.5 px ink. Action in 12.5 px/600 #B3161C with a 12 px icon. One sentence, one action, optional human fallback.
Copy rules
Say what happened in one plain sentence and what to do next. Never show status codes, stack traces, model names or the word "unexpected". Announce every notice through the existing live region so it isn't silent for screen readers.

Decisions log and remaining gaps
All three blocking questions are answered, so the design is complete enough to build. What remains are product defaults a developer can pick, and two states that were never drawn.
Resolved · confirmed by IPI

Product calls · pick a default, none block the build
Thumbs up / down — store where?
No feedback endpoint exists. Either keep it local per turn (visual only, no data) or add one. Don't invent an endpoint silently.
Retry — replace or append?
Re-asking the same question can overwrite the previous answer or add a second one below it. Appending is safer; replacing is tidier.
Raise MAX_SOURCES above 3?
The design handles any count. Leaving it at 3 means the "N more" state never appears — build it anyway, don't hardcode three.
Font delivery
Hanken Grotesk and Source Serif 4 are both free. Self-host or Google CDN — self-hosting avoids a third-party request on every page of the site.

Contrast floor. On white, text at 12 px or below uses #6E737B or darker. On a tinted fill (#F8F8F6, #F4F4F1) that is not enough — use #5C6169 or darker, placeholders included. #9099A4 is for borders and larger non-essential text only. Small red text uses #B3161C, never #EC1D24 — including section labels. Non-text cues — focus rings, state boundaries, control outlines — need 3:1 against their own background, so a focused field takes an ink #1B1E23 ring, not a pale grey one.
